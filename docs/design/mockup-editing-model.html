Command Center — on-canvas editing (dnd-kit)

Replaces the current flow (select → open the 772-line Inspector → find a size preset dropdown → no on-canvas delete or resize handle at all) with direct manipulation on the widget itself. Built on dnd-kit (already a dependency per the product-direction doc — just needs touch support added and a small resize-handle component paired with it) — additive to the existing canvas, not a rewrite. This editor is WYSIWYG: everything below is rendered true-to-scale, at the same relative size/position as it would appear on /display — the teacher edits a pixel-exact copy of the classroom TV, not an abstracted diagram of it. Feel is deliberately close to classroomscreen.com's direct-manipulation canvas (see the redesign doc's Part 2.0 research) — but this remains the /control-only copy; students on /display never see this editing chrome.

3 — Free placement on a snap grid: default zone highlight, plus automatic overlap prevention
Widgets are not confined to the 5 zone rects — a teacher can drag anywhere there's room on the underlying grid, and the drag snaps to the nearest grid line. Dropping near an empty default zone slot for that widget type still highlights and offers to snap to it (left panel below) — that's a helpful default, not a limit. What is a hard rule: dragging over a spot that would overlap another widget or group is blocked/nudged in real time (right panel below) — this, not confinement to 4 boxes, is what makes "no two widgets can overlap" a structural guarantee.

What moved where
Move → drag anywhere on the widget (touch + mouse), free placement on the snap grid, not confined to a fixed zone.
Resize → drag a corner handle (min/max clamped per widget type's floor, not per a fixed zone box).
Delete → 🗑 in the inline toolbelt, or a Delete-key shortcut.
Duplicate → ⧉ in the toolbelt.
Group → drag one widget onto another of a groupable type (e.g. timer onto timer) to combine them into one occupant; drag the group outline to move all members together.
Content edit (text/timer minutes/image) → double-tap → inline popover.
Widget-specific settings that don't fit inline (e.g. noise-game mode/level) → a short single-purpose sheet, not the 772-line Inspector.
What this removes
The always-open, 6-section, scroll-heavy Inspector for routine moves.
The size-preset-only dropdown (freeform resize now exists).
Mouse-only drag (onMouseDown/mousemove today, no touch) — becomes pointer-events based, works on iPad.
The duplicate "Send to Display" button question — one button, in the always-visible top bar (see control-home mockup).
The hard 4-widget-per-screen cap — replaced by "no overlap," enforced by the grid + collision check, with groups counting as one occupant (see the redesign doc, decision #4).
Any gap between what the editor shows and what the TV shows — the canvas above is WYSIWYG, not an abstracted mini-map.
